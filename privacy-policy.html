Appollo Studio ("Appollo Studio") has developed this Privacy Policy to help you understand how Appollo Studio collects, uses and discloses information Appollo Studio may collect from you and/or your device (computer, phone or other mobile device), or that you may provide to Appollo Studio, while using Appollo Studio's mobile applications Idle Cannon, Match Master and any of Appollo Studio. Collectively, the Apps and Websites are referred to as the “Appollo Studio Services."

For specific information on how Appollo Studio collects and uses information belonging to children under the age of 13 in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"), please refer to Section 10 of this Privacy Policy.

By using the Appollo Studio Services, you agree to how Appollo Studio collects, uses and discloses information that is collected from you. If you are under 18 years old, you acknowledge that your legal guardian has consented to this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy is an integral part of the Terms of Use If you, or your legal guardian do not accept or understand this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Appollo Studio Services.

If you have questions or complaints regarding Appollo Studio's Privacy Policy or practices, please contact Appollo Studio at marketing@zitga.com.vn.

1. DEFINITIONS

The following defined terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

"Financial Information" means information relating to payments and billing (e.g., name, account information, billing address) provided by you. All Financial Information is collected for billing and payment purposes only and will be protected and administered by Appollo Studio and/or our authorized third party partners.

"Non-Personal Information" means information that, by itself, does not identify a specific individual or entity (e.g., demographic information, usage data or downloads).

"Personal Information" means specific identifiable information about an individual or entity which may be used to identify, contact or locate that person or entity (e.g., name, mailing address, Device ID (defined below), email address or phone number).

2. WHAT INFORMATION IS COLLECTED?

Information You Provide. Appollo Studio may receive and store any Personal Information, Financial Information and/or Non-Personal Information that you provide when you interact with Appollo Studio. Some features and/or functionality of the Appollo Studio Services may be unavailable if you choose not to share certain information. Appollo Studio may use information you provide to communicate with you and to improve the Appollo Studio Services. Here are some examples:

Email Communications. Appollo Studio may use your Personal Information to send you newsletters and emails related to promotions and/or events. You can opt out of receiving promotional emails by following the instructions in the relevant correspondence. Once Appollo Studio has processed your opt-out request, Appollo Studio will not send you further promotional emails unless you later opt back in. Whether you opt out of receiving promotional emails or not, Appollo Studio reserves the right to email you with respect to important information, notices or changes relating to the Appollo Studio Services.

Contact Us. If you choose to use the "Contact Us" form you will be asked to provide Personal Information, request type of inquiry, and describe the purpose for which you are contacting Appollo Studio. If Appollo Studio is aware that you are under the age of 13, Appollo Studio will delete your email address once your request has been fulfilled.

Geolocation Data. If your location services are enabled, Appollo Studio may collect your geolocation information from the Pudding Monsters mobile application, but does not ask you for, access, or track any location based information at any time while downloading or using other Appollo Studio Services.

Push Notifications. On occasion, Appollo Studio will send you push notifications when using Appollo Studio's mobile applications regarding alerts to new high scores, as well as other account information that may be important to you. If you no longer wish to receive these types of communications, you may opt-out from receiving them by turning them off at the device level through the settings of the device or by contacting Appollo Studio at marketing@zitga.com.vn.

Other Information. You may provide other Personal and Non-Personal information whenever you interact with the Appollo Studio Services, including, without limitation, when you search the Appollo Studio Services, participate in a contest, survey or questionnaire, place an order, or communicate with Appollo Studio by phone or email.

With any of the above actions, you may supply Appollo Studio with information such as your name, address, phone number, alternate contact information, information about your use of the Appollo Studio Services, personal descriptions and product descriptions.

Information Collected Automatically or Through Technology. Appollo Studio may receive and store certain Personal Information and Non-Personal Information whenever you interact with Appollo Studio. While you are able to visit the Appollo Studio Services and remain anonymous, Appollo Studio and/or Appollo Studio's third-party service providers may still collect Non-Personal Information through technological means. Such information may be gathered by the following methods:

Tracking. Technologies such as cookies, beacons, tags and scripts are used by us and our partners, affiliates, or analytics or service providers. These technologies are used in analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking user's movements around the site and to gather demographic information about our user base as a whole. We may receive reports based on the use of these technologies by these companies on an individual as well as aggregated basis.

Device Information. When you download and use the Appollo Studio Services, Appollo Studio automatically collects information on the type of device you use, operating system, application version, and the persistent device identifier ("Device ID"). You consent to Appollo Studio collecting this information.

Metadata. Metadata is data collected and used to facilitate the Appollo Studio Services, monitor system usage, prevent fraud, and for other services. Some examples of metadata that Appollo Studio may collect and analyze include, without limitation, the Internet protocol (IP) address used to connect your computer to the Internet, computer and connection information (such as browser type, version, and time zone setting, browser plug-in types and versions, operating system, and platform), the full Uniform Resource Locator (URL) click sequence to, through, and from the Appollo Studio Services, including date and time, cookie number and any products, services or features you use or interact with arising out of or in connection with your use of the Appollo Studio Services.

Log files. As true of most websites, Appollo Studio gathers certain information automatically and stores it in log files. This information may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, operating system, date/time stamp, and/or clickstream data.

Tools. Appollo Studio may use software tools to measure and collect information about your use of the Appollo Studio Services, including, without limitation, page response times, upload and file transfer errors, interaction information (such as scrolling, clicks, and mouse- overs), features use, purchase tracking and order fulfillment and methods. Sometimes, you will have a choice whether or not to provide information, but if you fail to provide certain information it could impact some features and/or functionalities of the Appollo Studio Services.

Information from Other Sources. Appollo Studio may receive information about you from Appollo Studio's third-party service providers, or other sources such as purchase or redemption information, Appollo Studio Services usage, payment information, reporting information, page-view information, technical, fulfillment, advertising, search information and credit history information.

Mobile Analytics. Appollo Studio uses mobile analytics software to better understand the functionality of its Mobile Software on your phone. This software may record information such as how often you use the application, the events that occur within the application, aggregated usage, performance data, and where the application was downloaded from. Appollo Studio does not link the information stored within the analytics software to any personal information you submit within the application.

Local Storage Objects. Appollo Studio uses Local Storage Objects (LSOs) such as HTML 5 or Flash to store content information and preferences. Third-parties with whom Appollo Studio partners to provide certain features on Appollo Studio's site or to display advertising based upon your Web browsing activity use LSOs such as HTML 5 or Flash to collect and store information. To manage Flash LSOs, please click here:

3. HOW INFORMATION IS USED, SHARED AND DISCLOSED

Appollo Studio does not sell, and generally does not disclose, your Personal Information to third-parties without your consent. Except as set forth in Section 10 of this Privacy Policy, parents have the right to consent to collection and use of personal information from their child without also consenting to its disclosure to third-parties, as Appollo Studio does not share information that may be collected from your child. Appollo Studio uses Personal, Non-Personal and Financial Information, among other things, to monitor system performance, analyze internal business systems, improve the Appollo Studio Services, fulfill requests for additional products and services, contact you about promotional and marketing activities, provide customer support, and conduct research. The use and protection of information is an important component of Appollo Studio's business. Examples of how Appollo Studio shares and discloses your information is as follows:

Legal. Appollo Studio may disclose information you provide to respond to subpoenas, court orders, requests from authorities, and other legal process, and to establish/exercise Appollo Studio's legal rights. Appollo Studio may also disclose your information to defend against legal claims when Appollo Studio believes that disclosure is necessary to protect Appollo Studio's rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request. Appollo Studio may share Personal Information in order to investigate, prevent or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, and situations involving potential threats to the physical safety of any person, violations of Appollo Studio's Terms of Use, or as otherwise required by law.

Business Transfers. As Appollo Studio continues to develop its business, Appollo Studio may reorganize as a business entity, acquire entities, subsidiaries, or business units, or enter into joint ventures with third-parties. In any of these types of transactions, any information you provide may be one of the transferred business assets (subject to any pre-existing privacy notice unless you consent otherwise). In the event that all or substantially all of Appollo Studio's assets are acquired or Appollo Studio merges with a third-party, your information will likely be one of the transferred assets. You understand and agree that Appollo Studio reserves the right to transfer any information collected from you in the case of any such transfer. In such event, Appollo Studio will use reasonable efforts to notify you before Personal Information about you is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Affiliated Businesses We Do Not Control. Appollo Studio may use third-party companies and individuals to perform functions on Appollo Studio's behalf. Examples include fulfilling orders, processing payments, hosting, data storage, sending postal mail and email, removing repetitive information from customer lists, analyzing data, providing marketing assistance, providing search results and links (including paid listings and links), processing credit card payments, and providing customer service. Appollo Studio will provide such entities with access to certain information needed to perform their functions, but will take measures to ensure that they may not use it for other purposes. Appollo Studio shares information only as described above and with third-parties that are either subject to this Privacy Policy or to a privacy policy that is at least as protective as this Privacy Policy.

4. SECURITY

The security of your Personal Information is important to Appollo Studio. Appollo Studio follows generally accepted industry standards to protect the Personal Information submitted to Appollo Studio, both during transmission and once it is received. No method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure, however. Therefore, Appollo Studio cannot guarantee its absolute security. While no security system is completely secure, Appollo Studio and its third-party partners and vendors take appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access or disclosure of the information collected by Appollo Studio. For Appollo Studio, this includes, but is not limited to, ensuring that all Personal and Financial Information is disclosed only on a "need to know" basis and ensuring that all Personal Information collected by Appollo Studio is done so via secured connections and protected and encrypted (using secure socket layer technology,"SSL") by industrial grade security software to guard against unauthorized access.
